## 1. Description

Widen the v5 manifest schema `checkpoint` enums and their SPEC/MEM `subject`
conditionals so **new** approvals may use `AITL-<CODE>-Approval` while **migrated
history keeps `HITL-*`** (G36). This is the structural piece that lets BOLT-004's
renamed identifiers validate; it does not rename any identifier itself.

---

## 2. Expected outcome and completion evidence

- **Objective:** the shipped v5 schemas accept the AITL vocabulary for new
  approvals without rejecting the preserved `HITL-*` history.
- **Completion criterion:**
  - The `checkpoint` enum in each of the three `manifest-v5-{bolt,us,tc}.schema.json`
    gains the `AITL-*` names appropriate to that level (bolt: all applicable;
    us: `AITL-US-Approval`; tc: `AITL-TC-Approval`) **and keeps the `HITL-*`** names.
  - The Bolt schema's `subject`-shape conditionals (SPEC → requires `revision`;
    MEM → requires `v_bounce`) apply to **both** `HITL-SPEC/MEM-Approval` **and**
    `AITL-SPEC/MEM-Approval`.
  - The `mode`/approver rule (`mode: virtual` iff an approver is an `agent:` actor)
    is unchanged (it is checkpoint-name-independent).
- **Expected evidence:** a worked example manifest with one `AITL-*` entry and one
  historical `HITL-*` entry **validates GREEN**; a malformed variant
  (`AITL-SPEC-Approval` missing `subject.revision`) is **rejected RED**; `git status`
  shows only `distribution-kit/`.
- **Scope:** the three `manifest-v5-*.schema.json` files (enum + SPEC/MEM
  conditionals) + a validating worked example; a one-line note where §3.12 /
  `metrics/README` prose lists the enum, stating `AITL-*` is accepted (new) alongside
  `HITL-*` (history), **if** such prose exists.
- **Exclusions:** renaming identifiers in docs/examples (BOLT-004); the `HITL`
  adjective (BOLT-004); guardrails (BOLT-002, Done); the precept prose (BOLT-001,
  Done); enabling virtual approvers / registry / roster / pilot (later USs); the
  root `devflow/` (ADR-004).
- **Risks / controls:** breaking validation of existing (HITL-*) manifests →
  control: the AC keeps `HITL-*` in the enum and validates a historical entry;
  malformed AITL entry slips through → control: the AC rejects an `AITL-SPEC`
  without `subject.revision`.
