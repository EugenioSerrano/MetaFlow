---

## 1. Description

Remove every surviving copy of the risk-based approver-count rule that
`US-014.BOLT-003` was supposed to eliminate (BUG-002), so the distributable
stops contradicting itself about who approves the MEM. This Bolt is the
**first application of ADR-005**: completeness is proven by a phrase-family
sweep over the fixed location set, phrased as an absence — not by grepping the
locations it edits.

---

## 2. Expected outcome and completion evidence

- **Objective:** every distributable text that describes MEM approval states the
  approved single-approver rule (*one approver at any risk; additional
  QA/Sec/domain reviewers optional*), and no text states or implies the removed
  risk-based count.
- **Completion criterion:**
  - Zero occurrences of the removed rule's phrase family across the ADR-005
    location set, outside the allowlist declared in the SPEC.
  - The four agent definitions are self-consistent — their `HITL-MEM-Approval`
    table row **and** their V-Bounce step prose agree with the single-approver
    rule, and with each other.
  - The section titles that label the (now all-`1`) approver table no longer
    describe it as a risk rubric — those titles are members of the same phrase
    family (AREV-003 F-06.1), not a separate concern.
  - The **legitimate homonyms stay**: the escalation examples and role
    descriptions that mention QA/Sec as an optional control or a separate
    escalation concept are preserved verbatim (AREV-003 F-06.2). Over-removal is
    a failure of this Bolt, not a success.
- **Expected evidence (deterministic — class-1 defect, ADR-002):**
  - **RED:** the pre-fix phrase-family sweep showing the residual matches (the
    reproduction evidence in BUG-002 §2, independently reproduced three times
    during AREV-003).
  - **GREEN:** the post-fix sweep showing zero matches outside the declared
    allowlist, plus each carrier now stating the single-approver rule.
  - Four-agent whole-body diff = sanctioned divergence only; inline G-rule count
    **39** in GUARDRAILS and in each of the four agents.
  - `git status` shows only `distribution-kit/` files changed plus governance
    records — the root `devflow/` methodology content is untouched (ADR-004).
- **Scope:** every distributable text that states who approves the MEM or labels
  the approver table (the inventory is BUG-002 `affected_artifacts`; the SPEC
  enumerates files, the phrase family and the allowlist per ADR-005).
- **Exclusions:**
  - **AREV-003 F-02** (the no-holder fallback missing from two TC texts) — a
    residual of a *different* removal family (US-014.BOLT-001), therefore a
    separate defect with its own BUG and dedicated Bolt (G04, §2.16). Not fixed
    here.
  - The identity-separation rules (handoff incoming-executor, G37 Judge
    neutrality, G18/G24 no AI self-approval) — untouched by decision.
  - The root `devflow/` tree (receives the change at the next §5.16 migration).
  - Propagating the ADR-005 standard into the kit for adopters (deferred, ADR-005 §4).
- **Risks / controls:**
  - *Partial sweep again* (control: ADR-005 is now governing — the SPEC must
    declare the phrase family, the full location set and the allowlist, and the
    AC is an absence assertion; this is the fourth-occurrence guard).
  - *Over-removal of legitimate mentions* (control: the SPEC's explicit
    allowlist with a one-line reason per entry; an AC asserts they survive).
  - *Four-agent drift* (control: identical edits + whole-body diff + G-count).
  - *Root edit* (control: `git status` AC, kit-only per ADR-004).
