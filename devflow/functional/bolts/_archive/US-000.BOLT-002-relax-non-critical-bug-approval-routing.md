---

## 1. Description

Relax the `HITL-BUG-Approval` routing for **non-functional BUGs with
`severity: high|medium|low`**: any team member — **including the BUG's own
author** — may record the approval, so that a single-maintainer team is no
longer structurally blocked. The two other routes stay unchanged: functional
BUGs remain approved by a Functional Analyst, and non-functional BUGs with
`severity: critical` remain approved by an Architect or Tech Lead. The
dedicated Bolt of a non-functional BUG mirrors the relaxed route for
non-critical severities, so the fix at the BUG level is not re-blocked at the
Bolt level.

---

## 2. Expected outcome and completion evidence

- **Objective:** a team of any size — one person included — can complete the
  non-functional BUG route at non-critical severities through the governed
  lifecycle, with the approval still recorded under the approver's name, role,
  timestamps and evidence.
- **Completion criterion:** every text in the distributable that states the
  `HITL-BUG-Approval` routing for non-functional BUGs expresses the relaxed
  rule for `high|medium|low` and the unchanged rules for `critical` and for
  functional BUGs; no text still requires "a Developer other than the author"
  for non-critical non-functional BUGs or their dedicated Bolts.
- **Expected evidence:**
  - grep checks across the distributable showing the relaxed routing in every
    location that defines it (guardrail, methodology checkpoint table and
    severity-routing section, onboarding, affected templates and folder
    READMEs);
  - the four agent definitions' shared body in sync (whole-body diff, 2 lines
    of sanctioned divergence for the `agents-data` path) and their inline
    G-rule tables complete;
  - G-rule count invariant: 39/39/39/39.
- **Scope:** the guardrail and every distributable text that defines
  `HITL-BUG-Approval` routing for non-functional BUGs, including the
  dedicated-Bolt mirror.
- **Exclusions:** the functional route and the `critical` non-functional
  route (unchanged by decision); the other REV-001 role-availability findings
  (F-03..F-06: acceptance routing pairs, multi-approver counts, role
  multiplicity, SPEC/UAT counts) — they stay open and are not addressed here;
  no team-roster family; no changes to any other checkpoint's routing.
- **Risks / controls:** the main risk is governance-text drift across the four
  agent definitions and the documentation set — controlled by the sync-diff
  and count checks as mandatory completion evidence; a secondary risk is
  weakening review independence for non-critical defects — accepted by
  decision (REV-001 F-02) with the evidence requirement above as the
  compensating control.
