# US-014.BOLT-001 — Role routing as guidance, never a gate

---

## 1. Description

Implement the **role dimension** of US-014 in the distributable: state the
single-operator operability principle once as a governing default (D1), define
role multiplicity with recorded self-assignment (D2), and remove the
role-availability **block** from every named-role checkpoint while keeping the
role as guidance (D3) — preserving the identity-separation rules as the only
exceptions.

---

## 2. Expected outcome and completion evidence

- **Objective:** in `distribution-kit/`, no HITL checkpoint blocks for lack of
  a named-role holder; the named role remains stated everywhere as
  guidance/recommendation; the operability principle is a single governing
  statement from which the per-route texts derive.
- **Completion criterion:**
  - The operability principle (D1) is stated once as a governing default and
    referenced by the routing texts.
  - Role multiplicity + recorded self-assignment (D2) is stated where approvals
    are described.
  - Every single-role route in the US-014 §3 enumeration (methodology §3.0
    table, `GUARDRAILS.md` checkpoint map + work-category table, and the four
    agents' HITL tables) presents the role as guidance, not a gate (D3).
  - The identity-separation rules (handoff incoming-executor, G37 Judge-model
    neutrality, G18/G24) are explicitly **excluded** from the no-holder
    fallback and remain intact.
- **Expected evidence:** grep/consistency checks over `distribution-kit/`
  (principle present once; each enumerated route carries the guidance-not-gate
  treatment; identity rules unchanged); four-agent sync preserved (G-count and
  identical shared body); `git status` shows only `distribution-kit/` changed.
- **Scope:** D1, D2, D3 and the complete single-role-route enumeration
  (US-014 §3) — the *role* dimension only.
- **Exclusions:** D5 (AREV operability → US-014.BOLT-002); D7 (approver counts
  → US-014.BOLT-003); UNIT/UAT machinery (US-015); the team roster mechanism
  (US-001); the root `devflow/` tree (ADR-004 — next §5.16 migration).
- **Risks / controls:** a route missed in the enumeration (control: US-014 §3
  checklist + grep of every single-role Owner cell); the fallback weakening an
  identity rule (control: explicit exclusion list, verified); four-agent drift
  (control: identical shared-body edits + sync check).

---

## 3. Covered acceptance criteria

Covers these US-014 ACs (functional Bolt — parent ACs, quoted for readability):

- **AC-1** (US-014) — **Given** any HITL checkpoint and a team missing the
  named role, **When** an approval is due, **Then** the qualified human present
  may record it, capturing the self-assigned role (D1, D2).
- **AC-2** (US-014) — **Given** the role information in every text, **Then** the
  named role is still stated as guidance everywhere but never as a block (D1, D3).
- **AC (identity)** (US-014) — **Given** the identity-separation rules, **When**
  the no-holder fallback is applied, **Then** they are explicitly excluded and
  remain intact (D3).
- **AC (enumeration)** (US-014 §3) — **Given** the complete single-role-route
  enumeration, **Then** every route (including the four agents' HITL tables)
  carries the guidance-not-gate treatment.
- Partial **AC-3** (US-014) — the *role-block removal* half for the `critical`
  BUG route, the `infra`/`hardening` acceptance pairing and the two-role TC
  (the approver-count half of AC-3 is US-014.BOLT-003 / D7).
