# US-014.BOLT-003 — No risk-based approver counts

| Field        | Value |

---

## 1. Description

Implement **D7** of US-014 in the distributable: remove the risk-based
minimum-approver counts and make the executing DEV responsible for approving
the MEM, with SPEC and UAT requiring at least one approver.

---

## 2. Expected outcome and completion evidence

- **Objective:** in `distribution-kit/`, the risk-based minimum-approver table
  (MEM 1/1/2/3 by risk) is removed; **the DEV who takes the Bolt approves its
  MEM** (after a recorded handoff, the incoming executor); **SPEC and UAT
  require a minimum of one approver**; any additional named roles are guidance,
  not a required quorum.
- **Completion criterion:**
  - The §3.3 risk-based min-approver table and its GUARDRAILS mirror are
    replaced by the single-approver rule (executing DEV / incoming executor).
  - The §3.0 `HITL-SPEC-Approval` and `HITL-UAT-Approval` owner phrasing states
    a minimum of one approver.
  - The four agents' risk/approver tables are consistent with the above.
  - The identity-separation rules (handoff incoming-executor, G18/G24) remain
    intact — the DEV approving the MEM is the executor, never AI self-approval.
- **Expected evidence:** grep/consistency over `distribution-kit/` (no
  risk-based count survives; single-approver rule present; SPEC/UAT minimum
  one); four-agent sync preserved; `git status` shows only `distribution-kit/`.
- **Scope:** D7 only — approver counts.
- **Exclusions:** D1/D2/D3 (US-014.BOLT-001); D5 (US-014.BOLT-002); the root
  tree (ADR-004).
- **Risks / controls:** collision with BOLT-001 in the §3.3 MEM-approver area
  (control: **sequence after BOLT-001**; the SPEC reads the post-BOLT-001
  baseline); accidentally enabling AI self-approval (control: keep G18/G24 and
  the handoff rule explicit); four-agent drift (control: identical edits + sync
  check).

---

## 3. Covered acceptance criteria

- **AC-4** (US-014) — **Given** a Bolt taken by a DEV, **When** its MEM is due,
  **Then** that DEV approves it (incoming executor after a handoff), with no
  risk-based multi-approver requirement.
- **AC-5** (US-014) — **Given** SPEC and UAT approvals, **When** the count is
  checked, **Then** the minimum is one approver; extra roles are guidance.
- Partial **AC-3** (US-014) — the *approver-count* half for `high`/`critical`
  MEM (the role-block half is US-014.BOLT-001 / D3).
