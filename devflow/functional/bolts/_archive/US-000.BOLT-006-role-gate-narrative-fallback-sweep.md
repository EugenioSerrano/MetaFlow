## 1. Description

Complete the role-as-guidance change that `US-014.BOLT-001` (D1/D3) started:
bring the **normative narrative** of §3.0 — and §2.6.1 and `TEMPLATE-TC.md` —
into agreement with the §3.0 table, so no route statement gates a checkpoint on
a named role (BUG-003). This is the **second application of ADR-005**, in its
**positive-coverage** form: the sweep asserts that every route statement
*carries* the clause, not that a removed string is *absent*.

---

## 2. Expected outcome and completion evidence

- **Objective:** every text that states who records a HITL checkpoint reads as
  guidance, carrying the fallback clause approved in US-014.BOLT-001 ("or, if a
  named role has no holder, the available qualified human records it, noting the
  self-assigned role"), so a single operator is never blocked by the narrative.
- **Completion criterion:**
  - The 7 §3.0 narrative "Who:" bullets that state a named-role route
    (`HITL-US`, `HITL-BUG` functional route, `HITL-TC`, `HITL-BOLT-READY`,
    `HITL-ADR`, `HITL-SPEC`, `HITL-BOLT-DONE`) carry the clause or an explicit
    cross-reference to it; the §2.6.1 "Independent lifecycle" text and
    `TEMPLATE-TC.md` §10 likewise.
  - The DISC/REV/AREV bullets ("Qualified human designated for…") stay
    **untouched** — they are role-agnostic by construction and gate nothing;
    editing them would be over-correction.
  - The narrative agrees with the §3.0 table (the table already carries the
    clause — positive control).
- **Expected evidence (deterministic — class-1 defect, ADR-002):**
  - **RED:** the pre-fix positive-coverage sweep — enumerate every route
    statement (`grep -nA3 '**Who:**'` plus §2.6.1 and TEMPLATE-TC) and show 9
    lack the clause while the §3.0 table rows carry it (5/5).
  - **GREEN:** the post-fix sweep — every route statement carries the clause (or
    a cross-reference); zero gating narrative remains; the DISC/REV/AREV bullets
    unchanged.
  - Four-agent whole-body diff = sanctioned divergence only; inline G-rule count
    **39** in GUARDRAILS and each agent (this Bolt edits the methodology; the
    agents are checked to confirm they are **not** disturbed — and if any agent
    carries the same narrative, it is swept identically).
  - `git status` shows only `distribution-kit/` files plus governance records —
    root `devflow/` untouched (ADR-004).
- **Scope:** the route statements in `Avenga-DevFlow.md` §3.0 narrative (×7),
  §2.6.1, and `TEMPLATE-TC.md` §10 (BUG-003 `affected_artifacts`; the SPEC
  enumerates the exact lines and the clause wording per ADR-005).
- **Exclusions:**
  - BUG-002 / US-000.BOLT-005 (the risk-based approver-count family — a
    different phrase family, its own Bolt).
  - The identity-separation rules (handoff, G37, G18/G24) — untouched.
  - The root `devflow/` tree (next §5.16 migration).
- **Risks / controls:**
  - *Missed route statement* (control: ADR-005 positive-coverage sweep over the
    full location set — enumerate first, then assert coverage).
  - *Over-correction of role-agnostic bullets* (control: explicit AC that the
    DISC/REV/AREV bullets stay untouched).
  - *Four-agent drift* (control: identical edits where applicable + whole-body
    diff + G-count).
  - *Root edit* (control: `git status` AC, kit-only per ADR-004).
