# US-020.BOLT-001 — Manifest schemas + templates → v5

---

## 1. Description

Evolve the kit's three manifest **schema files** and their five **templates**
from v4.0 to v5.0: the approval array becomes `checkpoint_approvals[]` (replacing
`hitl_approvals[]`), recording actor + model + mode, and `schema_version` becomes
`"5.0"`.

---

## 2. Expected outcome and completion evidence

- **Objective:** the kit's manifest **schemas + templates** express the v5.0
  approval record.
- **Completion criterion:**
  - The three schemas (US / Bolt / TC) define `checkpoint_approvals[]` with, per
    entry: `checkpoint`, `subject`, `mode` (`human`|`virtual`), `decided_by`
    (`human:<user>` or `agent:<id>` with `model`), `decision`, `decided_at`
    (+ the review timings the family carries); `hitl_approvals[]` is gone.
  - `schema_version` constant is `"5.0"` in all three; `additionalProperties:
    false` preserved (missing OR extra field fails, G23).
  - The five `TEMPLATE-MANIFEST-*.json` reflect the v5 shape and validate
    against the v5 schemas.
- **Expected evidence:** a sample v5 manifest (one per level) validates green;
  a v4-shaped manifest (old `hitl_approvals[]`) fails against v5 (proving the
  change bites); `git status` shows only `distribution-kit/`.
- **Scope:** the three schema files + the five manifest templates in the kit.
- **Exclusions:** the methodology text §3.12/G23 and the four agents (BOLT-002);
  the §5.16 conversion path (BOLT-003); the checkpoint-name rename HITL→AITL
  (separate US — the field name `checkpoint_approvals` is neutral).
- **Risks / controls:** breaking the validator tool (US-012) — control: this
  Bolt only changes the schema/templates; the tool's alignment is US-012's
  concern, flagged in US-020 §5; schema-shape errors — control: sample-manifest
  validation in the SPEC's evidence.

---

## 3. Covered acceptance criteria

Covers the US-020 ACs about the schema and templates: `checkpoint_approvals[]`
replaces `hitl_approvals[]` in the three schemas with actor+model+mode;
`schema_version "5.0"`; `additionalProperties:false` preserved; the five
templates reflect and validate against v5. (Quoted from US-020 §1 — no new ACs
written here, §2.4.)
