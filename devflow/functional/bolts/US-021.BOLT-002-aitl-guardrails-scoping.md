## 1. Description

Evolve the **semantics** of the identity/naming guardrails for AITL, per ADR-008
§3.1 (G05 vocabulary) and §3.4 (G18/G24 scoped, not deleted; G37/handoff excluded
from no-holder fallback). This scopes *what the rules mean* under
human-by-default/agent-by-explicit-configuration — it does not add, remove or
renumber any rule, and it is not the mechanical identifier sweep (BOLT-004).

---

## 2. Expected outcome and completion evidence

- **Objective:** the guardrails express the AITL precept precisely — an AI actor
  may occupy an approval only under explicit valid configuration with independence,
  the record never fabricates a human, and the canonical vocabulary is `AITL-*`.
- **Completion criterion:**
  - **G05** — canonical checkpoint identifiers are `AITL-<CODE>-Approval`; `HITL-*`
    joins `H1–H6` as a **legacy** prefix (scoped, not honored as canonical going
    forward). (ADR-008 §3.1.)
  - **G18/G24** — scoped per ADR-008 §3.4: *"the AI never approves"* becomes *"the
    AI never approves **unless** the project has explicitly configured a valid
    virtual approver for that checkpoint class **and** the independence rule
    (approver actor ≠ executor actor) holds"*; a **virtual approval is always
    recorded as `agent:<id>` / `model:<id>`, never a human name**; the safe default
    (absent/invalid config → human-only) is preserved.
  - **G37 + handoff** — a note states they are **excluded from any no-holder
    fallback**: "no holder" may route a human role to another available human, but
    never licenses self-approval.
  - The **blocking-rule count stays 39** — text of existing rules evolves; **no
    rule is added, removed or renumbered**.
- **Expected evidence:** the scoped G05/G18/G24 text in GUARDRAILS + the four
  agents' guardrail table (byte-identical, G-count **39×5**); `git status` shows
  only `distribution-kit/`; the safe-default and identity guarantees are stated,
  not weakened.
- **Scope:** the **semantics** of G05, G18, G24 (+ a G37/handoff note) in
  `GUARDRAILS.md` and the four agents' compressed guardrail table.
- **Exclusions:** the mechanical `HITL-<CODE>-Approval` identifier sweep of other
  rules and incidental mentions (BOLT-004); the `HITL` adjective (BOLT-004); the
  schema enum (BOLT-003); the concept/precept prose (BOLT-001, Done); enabling
  virtual approvers / registry / Coordinator / roster / pilot (later USs); the
  root `devflow/` (ADR-004).
- **Risks / controls:** dissolving G18/G24 while scoping them → control: follow
  ADR-008 §3.4 verbatim (the record never fabricates a human; G37/handoff never a
  self-approval licence); miscounting rules → control: an AC asserts the count
  stays 39×5.
