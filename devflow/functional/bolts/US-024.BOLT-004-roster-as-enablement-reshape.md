---

## 1. Description

Reshape the kit's `actors/` family to the roster-as-enablement model fixed
by ADR-014 (§3.8–3.9) and deploy the mechanism's wording everywhere an
adopter can see it — the family docs, the methodology text and the four
MainAgents (the maintainer ADR is invisible to adopters; the kit is the
only place the norm exists for them).

---

## 2. Expected outcome and completion evidence

- **Objective:** the `actors/` family ships as: `README.md` (rewritten to
  the model) · `roster.yaml` (the team list — the single membership
  authority) · `roster.schema.yaml` (+ the v1 rule: `modes` contains
  `approver` ⇒ `approves` non-empty) · `TEMPLATE-ACTOR.yaml` (simplified —
  the `capabilities` block deferred to the v2 hardening) · `INDEX.md` ·
  `examples/` (the worked example moved there, its `definition:` pointer
  fixed to the `agents/squad/` path). **Retired:**
  `TEMPLATE-AITL-ENABLE-ADR.md` and `project-policy.yaml` (deleted).
- **Completion criterion:** the family matches ADR-014 §3.9 exactly; the
  methodology §3.0.1 names the human-authored roster grant instead of the
  AITL-enable ADR; `agents/roles/README` governance references updated; the
  four MainAgents define "explicit, valid configuration" inline as the
  schema-valid, human-authored roster entry (byte-identical across the
  four).
- **Expected evidence:** directory listing of `actors/`; the schema rule
  demonstrated (a malformed approver entry fails fast); the ADR-005
  phrase-family sweep over the kit for the retired mechanism → 0 remaining
  normative references; self-containment grep (0 maintenance IDs); the
  four-agent byte-sync + G-count check (US-016 discipline); no BOM.
- **Scope:** `distribution-kit/devflow/actors/**`, the two governance
  reference spots that today live in `agents/roles/README.md` — **edited at
  their post-split location** (`agents/README.md` / `agents/examples/`,
  after US-023.BOLT-005 executes first) — the methodology §3.0.1 line(s),
  and the shared-body clause in the four MainAgents.
- **Exclusions:** the `agents/` examples–squad split (US-023.BOLT-005); the
  MainAgent lifecycle capability (US-025); the v2 hardening (capability
  fields + schema-enforced ceiling + authority-change audit).
- **Risks / controls:** wording drift between the four MainAgents —
  controlled by the byte-sync check; a stale reference to the retired
  mechanism surviving — controlled by the phrase-family sweep (positive
  coverage, ADR-005).
